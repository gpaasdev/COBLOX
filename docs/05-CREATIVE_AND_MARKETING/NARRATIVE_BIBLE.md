# 📜 COBLOX Narrative Bible

> **The Lore and Worldbuilding Foundation**
> COBLOX is not just a sandbox; it is a world with history. This document ensures that every item description, environmental detail, and update fits within a cohesive sci-fantasy industrial universe.

---

## 1. The World: Aegis-VII

Aegis-VII is a shattered, tidally-locked planet. One side is scorched by a volatile sun, the other frozen in perpetual night. Life—and the game's setting—exists in the "Twilight Band," a rugged, cavernous ring where extreme temperatures compress the planet's core into powerful magical isotopes.

### The Central Conflict
It is not Good vs. Evil. It is **Reverence vs. Exploitation**.
- The ancient inhabitants worshipped the magical resources.
- The new arrivals want to mine them, refine them, and burn them for power.

---

## 2. The Factions

### The Sanctum (The Ancients)
- **Philosophy:** The magic (Aether) is a living, sacred entity. It must be guided, not forced.
- **Aesthetic:** Organic curves, floating geometry, white marble, gold inlays, and silent, glowing energy.
- **Status:** Extinct/Vanished. They left behind Relics, ruins, and dormant guardians.

### The Coven (The Industrialists)
- **Philosophy:** The planet is a battery. Magic is just another fuel source. If it resists, apply more pressure.
- **Aesthetic:** Heavy brass, cast iron, rivets, smoke, pistons, and chaotic, contained energy.
- **Status:** The player is an independent contractor, originally tied to the Coven, now building their own empire.

---

## 3. The Elements of Alchemy

Magic in COBLOX is treated as chemistry. There are four base states:

1. **Ignis (Combustion/Heat):** Volatile, red/orange. Used for smelting and power generation.
2. **Aqua (Flow/Cooling):** Fluid, deep blue. Used for lubrication, cooling hyper-threaded machines, and hydraulic pressure.
3. **Terra (Structure/Mass):** Dense, green/brown. Used for chassis integrity and heavy plating.
4. **Void (Aether/Anti-gravity):** Purple/Black. The rarest state. Defies physics, used for teleportation and spatial compression (e.g., larger inventories).

---

## 4. Key Technology

- **The Arcane Boiler:** The foundational technology. Burns conventional fuel to heat water, but the steam is passed through an Aether crystal, creating "Magi-Steam," which powers everything.
- **The Crucible:** A heavy containment vessel designed to force two opposing elements (like Ignis and Void) to fuse without exploding.

---

## 5. Writing and Dialogue Style

- **No Slang:** Characters and descriptions do not use modern internet slang.
- **Tone:** Technical, slightly clinical, but with an undercurrent of wonder or dread.
- **The "Found Footage" Approach:** Lore is delivered through fragmented blueprints, logbook entries of deceased Coven engineers, and cryptic Sanctum engravings.

### Examples of Voice
*Coven Engineer Log:* 
> "Valve 4 blew again. The brass melted like wax. Whatever is inside that Voidstone, it doesn't want to be contained. I've ordered thicker plating. We will break it."

*Sanctum Engraving:* 
> "To bind the wind is to kill it. Let the Aether flow, and it shall sing."
