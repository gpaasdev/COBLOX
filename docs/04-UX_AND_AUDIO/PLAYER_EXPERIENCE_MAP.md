# 🗺️ COBLOX Player Experience Map

> **The Player's Journey**
> We do not just design screens; we design emotions over time. This map tracks the player's journey from the moment they discover COBLOX to their long-term retention phase.

---

## 1. The Funnel Stages

### 1.1 Discover (Off-Platform / Storefront)
- **Goal:** Catch the eye, communicate AAA quality, and promise deep progression.
- **Desired Emotion:** Curiosity, Intrigue.
- **Friction:** Generic thumbnails, confusing titles.
- **Metrics:** CTR (Click-Through Rate), Impressions.

### 1.2 Join & Loading
- **Goal:** Seamlessly transition the player into the world while preloading essential assets.
- **Desired Emotion:** Anticipation.
- **Friction:** Long load times, abrupt popping of assets.
- **Metrics:** Load time (Target: <5 seconds), Join-to-Play ratio.

### 1.3 Intro & FTUE (First Time User Experience)
- **Goal:** Teach the core loop (Harvest -> Refine -> Craft) without overwhelming menus.
- **Desired Emotion:** "Aha!" Moment, Empowerment.
- **Friction:** Wall of text, confusing controls, lack of direction.
- **Metrics:** D1 Retention, Tutorial Completion Rate.

### 1.4 Early Game (Harvest & Basic Crafting)
- **Goal:** Establish the rhythm of manual labor before introducing automation.
- **Desired Emotion:** Satisfaction of hard work.
- **Friction:** Tedium (if it lasts too long).
- **Metrics:** Time to first machine built.

### 1.5 Mid Game (Automation & Exploration)
- **Goal:** Shift from manual labor to logistical management. Introduce deeper biomes.
- **Desired Emotion:** Flow-state, Pride in efficiency.
- **Friction:** Spaghetti belts, confusing pipe logic, inventory management.
- **Metrics:** Average Session Length, D7 Retention.

### 1.6 Late Game (Bosses & Arcane Research)
- **Goal:** Test the player's logistical might against environmental threats and massive crafting requirements.
- **Desired Emotion:** Mastery, Epic scale.
- **Friction:** Resource drought, server lag from too many machines.
- **Metrics:** D30 Retention, Boss Defeat Rate.

### 1.7 Elder Game (Prestige & LiveOps)
- **Goal:** Give players a reason to reset or participate in weekly challenges.
- **Desired Emotion:** Dedication, Community belonging.
- **Friction:** Burnout, lack of meaningful updates.
- **Metrics:** LTV (Life Time Value), DAU/MAU ratio.

---

## 2. Core Loop Emotional Sine Wave

A perfect session should not be a flat line of activity. It should look like a sine wave:
1. **High Intensity (Action):** Fighting a biome hazard, expanding a base rapidly.
2. **Low Intensity (Waiting/Planning):** Watching machines process, optimizing belts, reading lore.
3. **Climax (Reward):** Unlocking a new tier, crafting a legendary item.

*Rule:* If the player is in "High Intensity" for too long, they burn out. If they are in "Low Intensity" for too long, they get bored. Design the pacing to naturally force oscillation between the two.
